Import your Instagram saves

There is no official API that gives a third-party app your saved posts — Meta simply doesn't expose them. So My Archive syncs the honest way: you export your own data from Instagram and import it here. The file never leaves this machine.

  1. In Instagram, go to Settings → Accounts Centre → Your information and permissions → Download your information.
  2. Choose JSON format (not HTML) and request the download.
  3. When it arrives, find saved_posts.json inside the download and drop it below.

Every post is imported with its link, author, caption and save date, then sorted into a genre automatically. Posts already in your archive are skipped as duplicates — importing the same export twice is safe.
